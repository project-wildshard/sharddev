# Mossgate Hollow golden targets

> Written by: Claude (opus-goldtargets)

> **State:** look targets, made 2026-11-11 (Cancún) for TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN row LF-T1 in project-wildshard-meta
> `docs/plans/TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN.md`. Mockups, not renders: nothing here is built yet.

Twenty golden target images for Template 1, Mossgate Hollow v3 (swords, sunset-to-dusk sky, lantern and brazier loop, moss bridge, Barrow Warden). Each is a first-person frame in our engine's camera and
HUD, one step past the current build and still a lightweight dev-test level. The look loop
(singleplayer `docs/design/LOOK-LOOP.md`) anchors to them: build a zone, capture the same camera, measure the gap.

These are art, not product: keep them out of `src/shards/_template/` and out of any shipped pack.

## How they were made

Codex CLI `codex exec` with its built-in image_gen, one generation per file (1536x1024 desktop, 1024x1536
pillarboxed phone, cropped to its screen and resampled to 402:874), from three image inputs: a real engine shot of this level for
camera, scale and HUD, plus the approved v3 board mockups for style. Every image was looked at and
re-rolled when it read as concept art or broke the camera or HUD. Desktop files are 1400x933 JPEG;
phone files are 706x1536 JPEG (402:874).

Every prompt is the common block (engine camera and HUD), then the style paragraph below, then the shot.
Phone shots add the phone-frame paragraph. The exact prompts are in `prompts.json`.

## Style paragraph

STYLE "Mossgate Hollow" (Template 1): a small mossy hollow at sunset turning to dusk. Floor of moss-green tiles with faint dev-grid seams; bevelled mossy grey-stone walls with fuzzy moss caps and small stencilled size labels like "3x3"; a stone hut with a moss-shingle roof and warm wooden door posts; three stone braziers A, B and C on stencilled pads; round toy trees; clumps of low-poly ferns and grass tufts along wall bases, little red-capped mushrooms, mossy boulders, a path of flat stone slabs, hanging rope lanterns. Warm amber lantern and brazier glow with soft light pools, gentle rim light, contact shadows, a few drifting firefly sparks, thin ground mist in low spots. SKY: a sunset gradient, warm peach-gold at the horizon through rose into deep dusky violet-blue at the top, soft clouds lit pink and gold from below; the haze tinted warm peach. Characters are chunky and toy-like with simple two-dot eyes. The player's weapon is an IRON SWORD (clean low-poly blade, leather-wrapped grip, simple brass crossguard), held in first person at the lower right with the blade rising toward the centre, like the long held item in image 1. Hotbar slots: "LANTERN" and "IRON SWORD".
