# 2026-11-13: the generic far bake drops its impostor over the cap

> Written by: Claude

- `wildshard check` bakes a far proxy for a shard with a terrain and
  `far: null` (`projectPlaceholders`). The GLB plus its impostor broke
  the far resident cap on Driftwood (1,605,724 B vs 1,600,000 B) and
  Pine Hollow (1,631,500 B), so their checks always refused (meta
  QA-FEEDBACK-2026-11-13 row 4, SHARD-AUTHORING SA11).
- `projectFarPlaceholder` now keeps the GLB alone (the proxy draws at
  every distance) when the pair is over a far cap, as
  `authoredFarImpostor` already did, and warns; it refuses only when
  the GLB alone is over. `bakeFarPlaceholder`, which the repo runners
  call, still refuses. `far-cap.test.ts` covers each case, the caps passed in.
- Engine's `test/driftwood-scene.test.ts` pins the old refusal on its
  own copy of `placeholders.ts`; that test is the coordinator's.
