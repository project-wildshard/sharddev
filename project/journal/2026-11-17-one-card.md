# One card (ONE-FORMAT OF6)

- OF6a (opus-of-m6d): `shardCardFile(source)` in build's `shard-outputs.mjs` (and `shardCardFile` / `shardCardPath` in
  upload's `shardSource.ts`) names a product by its card: a legacy `manifest.json` while one is left, else
  `data/card.json`. The product list, the src/ slug, output composition, the build's manifest intake, the hero capture
  and stamp read through it. Proof: with `_template/manifest.json` moved aside, `build-shards --shard=_template` builds
  and `discovery.json` is byte-identical. `gen-shards` / `push-tables` stay on the engine's `authored-manifest.mjs`,
  which reads `manifest.json` itself (engine trees only).
