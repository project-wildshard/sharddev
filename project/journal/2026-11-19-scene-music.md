# world.music and world.ambience

Meta MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ23 (opus-mossgate-m6, 2026-11-19). A scene shard kept the format's silent score, which zeroes the
music bus in its cell, so quest-step stings were never heard (QA 11-19 row 35, "nothing happens"). The scene SDK now
declares a score of the engine's piano slots (no shard audio bytes, J304) and a synth wind bed; either sets
`score: default`. The engine half (a grid data cell plays the score, day/night/boss) is engine 30c22f00.
