# A quest beat lands where it happened

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossgate-m7), 2026-11-19

Meta MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ31, QA 11-19 row 35 (Jake in Glowfen: "I'm setting these lanterns and nothing happens. It's just
so anticlimactic."). `world.questStep(quest, step, node, options)` and `world.questPayoff(quest, node, options)` place a
quest's beats in the scene, as format 0.56's step `feedback` and `onComplete.payoff`; the engine (a394a616a) flares the
spot, spreads light over the ground, pops sparks and plays one of its own synth voices. A payoff's `reveal` names a
shared flag its completion scene raises, so a panel can show a change that was never there. Mossgate's braziers flare
amber with `ignite`, each leg of the old rite glows where it is read, and the Lamplighter's chain lights a ring of six
lanterns round her, with the score's dawn take and the Pond Hermit's gate as the next goal. Every format pin is 0.56.0.
