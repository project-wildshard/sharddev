# Mossgate Hollow golden targets

> Written by: Claude (opus-goldtargets), 2026-11-11 ~21:15 Cancún

Jake asked for a corpus of 20 golden images per template, made with the
Codex CLI in our game's style, to set the look target the template lanes
build toward (meta TEMPLATE-E2E-PLAN, Look fidelity, row LF-T1).

- `art/targets/mossgate/`: 20 first-person JPEGs (16 desktop, 4 phone at
  402:874), named zone then camera, each in our engine's camera and HUD.
- `README.md` lists every file with its zone, camera and shot prompt;
  `prompts.json` has the full prompts and image inputs.
- Art, not product: nothing here is read by the build or shipped, and it
  stays out of `src/shards/_template/`.
