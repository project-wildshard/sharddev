# Mossgate camps to the bar

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossgate-m5), 2026-11-19

MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ21. The full-zone bar asks for 45+ bodies in 8 camps that come back. Two camps join, both off the main
chain's path: five grey blobs on Lookout Ridge's lower slope (70 m south of the Lookout; the slope's pines keep clear),
and five barrow crawlers round the Old Cairn's east foot. A third sow fills the Sow Wallow, and the pond's sows and
wisps respawn like every other camp. 46 bodies, 8 species, ten clusters of three or more. `wildshard content` has no
camp count (its `groups` are pack and herd brains), so `mossgate.test.ts` counts camps by clustering bodies 15 m apart.
