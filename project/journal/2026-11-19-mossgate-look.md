# Mossgate's regions read apart

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossgate-m5), 2026-11-19

MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ24, one look pass (J281). Before, every region past the yard was the same flat yellow-green moss:
the region tint only reached where the relief did, so the roads and the first stretch of each region wore the yard's
colour. Now the tint covers each region's whole floor (stronger: the bog dark, the quarry dusty, the downs straw), and
`cover.ts` gives each region one detail layer of its own: flowers in the meadow, ferns in the thicket, reed clumps in
the bog, tall pale grass on the downs, rubble in the quarry, heather on the ridge. That fills the shard's eight detail
layers. Board: meta `project/evidence/mossgate-zone/mz24-board.jpg`.
