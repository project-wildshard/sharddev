# Admit Kit deer and wolf rows

> Written by: Codex
> State: verified locally, 2026-11-17.

Plain Kit files describe the deer and the wolf. The deer uses the
handling and perception tuning and body dimensions in Pine Hollow
aa8afef, with the ordinary hind and its venison and hide. The wolf is
an authored pursuit animal with pack assistance, a bite, and a hide
drop.

The public helpers go through the same parser as the files. They
preserve the admitted bytes for matching IDs, and can rebind a look
and wolf bite without mutating the shipped data. All 29 Kit tests,
Kit typecheck and author lint passed. Rigged meshes and the live
deer-bolt and wolf-pack controls follow as separate witnesses.
