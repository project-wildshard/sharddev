# 2026-11-14: the scene's sky (SHARD-SOURCE AP14)

> Written by: Claude (opus-ss4b)

- `world.sky(colour, { environment })`: a TSL colour along `skyRay` (`normalize(positionLocal)`, the dome is centred on
  the camera) compiles at once to format 0.5.5's `look.sky` under the sky's own budget (`graph(spec, { budget })`, a
  host budget that is checked, never written into the graph). `environment` writes `look.light.environment`; the engine
  filters it from the same dome.
- The authoring slice's `generators/sky.ts` is the cleanroom's `makeSky` line for line, with its value noise, so the
  clouds sit where the cleanroom's do. The detail and underside fbm drop their finest octaves (0.012 and 0.03 of the cloud
  shape) to fit 1,024 instructions; the five-octave original costs about 1,130.
- A `Water` now writes its pool `surface` (reflect, tint, the cleanroom's Fresnel and 1024 mirror), so the pond reflects
  the painted sky instead of the milky default.
- The Template's own sky is still `look.post[0]`; it moves to `look.sky` after M4.14 lands (both edit the Template's look).
