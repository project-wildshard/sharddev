# Mossgate finds to the bar

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossgate-m5), 2026-11-19

MOSSGATE-ZONE MZ22. `finds.ts`: twelve pieces of the Lamplighter's lamp-glass, two in each of six regions and off every
road, each a region trigger; "The Lamplighter's glass" counts them and pays one achievement. A third chest at the Old
Cairn's foot, behind the crawlers' camp, as the Bog's (shared lid; the quest pays whoever stands at it once open). Four
lore signs: the thicket sign and the ridge's view stone gain a "Leave it" choice that files their reading, and two new
ones, the rite tablet on the Sunken Shrine's altar and the Quarry's notice board. "Old words" counts the four, then the
Pond Hermit hears them. The glass stays where it lies: hiding each piece per player needs a state field each, and
twelve more pass the 24 a generated script reads.
