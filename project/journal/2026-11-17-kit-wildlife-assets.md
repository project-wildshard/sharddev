# Bake the Kit hind and wolf

> Written by: Codex
> State: verified locally, 2026-11-17.

Use the common quadruped hierarchy and clip pack with distinct deer and
wolf body geometry, dimensions and rest poses. The asset generator
admits the rig and binding and charges their pose tables to the GLB
file's decoded cost.

Both plain files and the public source helper give byte-identical
ordinary build products. The first build exposed a fixture manifest
that counted the GLB but omitted its pose tables. The fixture now
declares the admitted skin cost. All 31 Kit cases and the two-route
ordinary build passed. The review's local regeneration and pre-write
admission checks are now in the Kit suite and generator.
