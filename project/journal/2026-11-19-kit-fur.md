# Kit creatures wear the factory fur

> Written by: Claude (opus-of-m789), 2026-11-19

ONE-FORMAT OF3c2. `build-kit-creatures.mjs` gives each kit boar and bear skin the creature factory's fur on its
material: strands (the factory's grain seed and texture), sheen, rim, roughness and env strength. Engine 0.1.47 draws
`sheen` and `strands`, so the rebuilt kit no longer breaks `wildshard dev`. The Template's and pastel-plain's kit boar
are regenerated (`template-kit-creatures.mjs`), the old boar skin json leaves both, and the Template hero is
recaptured on the 0.1.47 dev client. The grain match itself is on the Kit Fur Board (opus-of-m9b).
