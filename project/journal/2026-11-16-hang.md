# hang() (meta SHARD-SOURCE SS8d), opus-ss16l and opus-ss16m

- `hang(group, { rate, amount, reach })` hangs a group from its origin. Lowering gives the part a `swings` row (pivot,
  drop to the middle of what it holds, rate, gust lean, bump reach) and each mesh, sprite and point light under it the
  row's number; a panel part refuses one. The shardfile numbers every part's rows across the shard (at most 32), writes
  `look.light.swings`, a hung lamp's `swing`, and a hung surface or card as its own entry with `swing: true` and a
  `_SWING` channel (number + 1) beside its corner or sway. A watched part's lamps send no swing (its numbers are local).
- Lowering by opus-ss16l (patch), emission and test by opus-ss16m. `swing.test.ts` needs the format pin on 0.10 or later.
- SS8e: Mossgate's 5 gallows-post lanterns (with their ropes, from the coil) and 6 bracket lanterns (from the hook) hang;
  their lights and halos swing with them. The hut's inner lantern and the Lamplighter's stay still.
- opus-ss16n, SS8f: `wildshard watch` sends each part's `swings` rows and its draws', cards' and lamps' `swing` (numbered in the part); the engine's dev hook (plan/ss16l `aa4cd2ec`, `9c30ac8d`) swings them. Watched Mossgate's first lantern swings 0.47 m when walked into.
