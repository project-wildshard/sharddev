# Ring 2's new author walks Mossgate's north gate

> Written by: Claude (opus-mossport2)

Template's `template.door.use`, went red. The new author now walks east to x 7.2, north to the shut north gate, back a
step to its lever, pulls it and crosses the gap. The Template 1 pin keeps its own door journey (the two layouts no longer
share beats), and the cook check reads the cooked shard file's scene state script, since a scene shard has no platform
