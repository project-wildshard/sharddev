> Written by: Codex
> **State:** built, 2026-11-16; ONE-FORMAT M1 OF31b2a.

Kit content preserves the former six deterministic drop tables and their
variant predicates. Its build-only compatibility helper writes ordered
item/count/variant declarations over the source's explicit roster. It
never draws RNG, mutates the roster or shares a result with another call.

mutually exclusive monkey fur and exact versus substring selectors.

admission integration waits in OF31b2b for the new format release; its
format-owned declaration type replaces this intermediate output type.
